➡️ PART 5 · LO QUE VIENE 8 min

You have just built the foundation for two modules

Módulo 4 takes everything from this week and points it at a friend instead of a stranger. The theme is consejos entre amigos, and the grammar is the tú command — including its negative, which is built from exactly the forms you learned on Days 3 and 5. No tome becomes no tomes. One letter.

And the pronoun rule transfers untouched: attached to an affirmative (dímelo), in front of a negative (no me lo digas). Same principle, same tildes, same order.

💡 Y AHORA LO IMPORTANTE
The forms you spent this week on — tome, siga, cruce, vaya, sea, dé, esté, sepa — are the subjunctive. In Módulo 7 they come back with a different job, and nothing new is added to them. You already produce every form the mood needs.

What Módulo 7 teaches is when: after quiero que, after es necesario que, after te recomiendo que. The morphology is finished. Only the reason is new.

That is why Módulo 3 sits where it does, and why this page is telling you so rather than saving it as a surprise. A student who can produce no cruce and tómelo automatically in October has already done most of the work for November.

🌎 CULTURA · COLOMBIA
Lo que te llevas de este módulo

Four things from Colombia worth keeping. Bogotá is calculated and Cartagena is walked — a numbered grid at 2,600 metres versus a walled colonial town where you navigate by a yellow church. No dé papaya, the most Colombian expression there is, and a negative command. Con mucho gusto and a la orden, a register of everyday courtesy visitors notice immediately.

And the correction to a rule this course gave you earlier: in much of Colombia usted is used with family and close friends. Distance-versus-closeness is a good default across the Spanish-speaking world, not a law. When a Colombian addresses you as usted, it may be warmth.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA CORTESÍA

Ten days on the grammar of telling people what to do, in a module whose virtue is courtesy — which is not the contradiction it looks like. Every helpful person in this module used a stream of imperatives: the receptionist, the ticket seller, the fruit vendor in Cartagena, the guide writer. What made them courteous was never avoiding the command. It was the reason attached, the warning volunteered, the alternative offered, the cuídese at the end. Next module the virtue becomes el servicio, and the same lesson holds. La cortesía no está en el mandato, sino en lo que lo acompaña.
