Lesson 4.09
★ Tarea oral — una nota de voz con un consejo de verdad
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A voice note is spoken to one person. That is the whole difference, and it changes how it should sound.
Today you record the Tarea oral (20 pts) — 20% of your semester grade. The content is already chosen: yesterday's Part 5 asked you to pick the friend and the situation.

The format is a nota de voz, the medium Dominicans and most of the Spanish-speaking world use for exactly this. It is not a presentation. Nobody performs a voice note.

The specific technical challenge of this module is stress. Dímelo is one word with the stress at the front; no me lo digas is four words with the stress in the same place. Get that wrong and a listener loses the verb, which is the one thing your advice cannot afford.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to speak for 60–90 seconds giving connected advice in tú commands.
2
I will be able to pronounce an attached pronoun and a negative command with the stress in the same, correct place.
3
I will be able to include a warning and a reason without sounding like I am lecturing.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

Six words, not a script

Same method as every module: a six-word outline, never a written-out text. A read script always sounds read — and with advice it is worse than usual, because the quality that makes advice land is sounding like somebody who has actually been in the situation. Nobody reads a voice note aloud to a friend.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Say «dímelo» aloud. Which syllable did you land on?
🎤 PART 2 · LA TAREA 9 min

El tema: Grábale una nota de voz a un amigo o a una amiga que te ha pedido consejo.

A real situation, either a decision they are facing or something practical you know and they do not. Speak to one person, by name if you like. The best version of this task is the one you would genuinely send.

Not scored: a native accent. Comprehensibility is scored; sounding Dominican is not, and this course does not teach you to. The one pronunciation feature that does count is the stress inside an attached-pronoun command, because misplacing it makes the verb unrecognisable — a comprehension problem, not an accent one.

🧠 PART 3 · TU GUION DE SEIS PALABRAS 9 min

Six beats, six words. These are 4.06's five moves, arranged for speech.

Beats 4 and 5 are both assessed — beat 4 by command accuracy and range, beat 5 by discourse shape. Write those two words first. A recording that is nothing but affirmative instructions forfeits points in two separate criteria, and it is also the version your friend is least likely to act on.

⭐ ENSAYA TRES VECES
Say the whole thing aloud three times from your six words — not from sentences. On run three, listen for two specific things: the stressed syllable at the front of any attached pronoun, and whether beat 5 actually came out as a reason rather than as one more instruction.

If run three is shorter than 60 seconds, the missing time is almost always beat 5. A reason takes two sentences, not four words.
🔊 PART 4 · PRONUNCIACIÓN DE LOS MANDATOS 11 min

Click each and repeat aloud, twice.

  • di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, venone syllable each — land the vowel
  • dímelo, ayúdame, levántatestress at the front, where the tilde is
  • no hables, no comas, no vayastwo separate words, both audible

1. The stress does not move when the word breaks apart. DÍ-me-lo → no me lo DI-gas. Same syllable, both times. If you can hear that, you can hear the whole system: the tilde appears only when the pronouns are glued on, but the stressed syllable was never in doubt.

2. The eight irregulars are one syllable and vanish if rushed. Ten. Pon. Sal. Ve. Give each a small landing — a fraction of a pause after it — or your listener hears a vowel and guesses. This is the single easiest way to make a spoken command followable.

3. A negative command is not one word. No te preocupes has gaps in it. Running it together as “notepreocupes” is the audible equivalent of writing it joined up, and it is where the no gets lost — which reverses your advice.

✅ TRY IT · what the rubric actually weighs
Two takes. Take A has an obvious US accent but includes «no le escribas todavía» and two sentences explaining what happened to you last year. Take B is smooth and confident, all affirmative commands, no reason. Which scores higher?
⏺ PART 5 · GRABA Y ENTREGA 6 min

Record in Canvas on the assignment itself. Then listen once — all the way through, not in pieces — and check four things:

  1. Are there at least two negative commands, and is each one a Módulo 3 form with an -s?
  2. Did your attached pronoun land its stress at the front of the word?
  3. Is there a sentence that gives a reason, in a past tense?
  4. Did you reach 60 seconds? Short takes are nearly always missing beats 5 and 6.

If all four pass, submit. If one fails, re-record — takes are unlimited and only your best is graded, so there is no reason at all to submit a take you know is short of a criterion.

🌎 CULTURA · REPÚBLICA DOMINICANA
La bachata también habla de tú

The Dominican Republic has given the world two musical forms, and both are Dominican in origin rather than merely Dominican in popularity: el merengue and la bachata. UNESCO inscribed the music and dance of merengue on its Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2016, and Dominican bachata in 2019.

Bachata's history is worth knowing. For decades it was rural, poor and socially looked down on — dismissed as música de amargue, “bitterness music”, and largely kept off the radio. It travelled anyway, carried by migration and by cheap recordings, and by the 1990s artists including Juan Luis Guerra had moved it into the mainstream. It is now one of the most widely listened-to Latin genres on earth.

Here is why it belongs on today's page. Bachata is almost always sung in the second person, to one named absent person: dime, no me digas, escúchame. It is the grammar of this whole module, set to a guitar — and it is doing the same thing your voice note does. One person, addressed directly, told something that matters.

★ LA VIRTUD · EL SERVICIO

Beat 5 is the serviceable one and it is also the uncomfortable one, because the most useful reason you can offer is usually a time it went badly for you. Encouragement is cheap and accuracy costs something. Rehearsing three times so a friend understands you the first time is the other half of the same virtue: nobody will ever see the rehearsal. Grabar otra vez cuesta cinco minutos; entender mal un consejo cuesta mucho más.
