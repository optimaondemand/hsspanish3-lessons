⭐ PREPARACIÓN · eight minutes, now
Reopen 4.01 Part 2 and 4.02 Part 2. Play every 🔊 with your eyes closed and say the meaning aloud before you look at it. Then write these three from memory and check every accent: la recomendación · la solución · tener razón.

All three end in a stressed -ón, which is exactly the ending English speakers stop marking. If typing them is awkward, open the Cómo escribir acentos en español page linked in this module and in the item's description.

🌎 CULTURA · REPÚBLICA DOMINICANA
Palabras taínas — el vocabulario que viajó al revés

On a day about vocabulary, here is some that went the other way. The taínos were the indigenous people of Hispaniola — the island the Dominican Republic shares with Haiti — and Spanish absorbed a set of their words early, then carried them into English and most other European languages.

You already use several: huracán (hurricane), canoa (canoe, often cited as the first Amerindian word written down in Spanish), barbacoa (barbecue), hamaca (hammock), maíz, tabaco, iguana, sabana.

The layer is thickest in the Dominican Republic itself, where a great many place names remain Taíno — Samaná, Higüey, Baní, Jarabacoa — and where casabe, flatbread made from cassava, is still eaten under its Taíno name. So the twenty words you are about to be tested on are the newest layer of a language that has been borrowing from this island for five hundred years.

★ LA VIRTUD · EL SERVICIO

Revising rather than rewriting is the serviceable choice here, and not only because it is faster. The paragraph you are submitting is addressed to somebody with a decision to make — so the question the rubric is really asking is whether a person could use it. Clear beats impressive; a reason beats an instruction. Un consejo sirve solo si el otro puede usarlo.
