★ Repaso del módulo — el Grammar Quiz y lo que viene
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Two modules on commands close today. Nothing in the rest of the semester adds a single new command form.
Last day of Módulo 4. Work the review, take the Grammar Quiz (20 pts, two attempts, includes listening), and read Part 5 properly — because it does two things. It introduces Módulo 5, which changes subject completely, and it settles what you now own: the entire command system of Spanish, in both registers, affirmative and negative, with pronouns.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to build any tú command, affirmative or negative, with or without pronouns, without hesitating.
2
I will be able to decide correctly whether a form needs a tilde, by counting syllables rather than guessing.
3
I will be able to explain what Módulo 5 asks of me, and why Módulo 7 adds no new verb forms.

Take attempt 1 seriously — its real value is telling you which of the twelve areas above is weak — then reopen that lesson before attempt 2. Students who take both attempts back to back usually score about the same twice, having learned nothing in between.

One warning specific to this quiz: some items give you two forms that are both perfectly good Spanish and ask which one says what you mean. Those cannot be answered by pattern-matching a signal word. Read the situation, not the sentence.

➡️ PART 5 · LO QUE VIENE 8 min

You have finished the command system

Count what Módulos 3 and 4 gave you together: usted and ustedes commands, tú commands, affirmative and negative in both registers, pronouns attached and detached, and the tilde rule that governs all of it. That is the complete imperative of Spanish. Nothing later in this course adds a new command form.

Módulo 5 changes subject entirely. The theme is el medio ambiente — the environment — the country is Ecuador, with the Galápagos at the centre of it, and the virtue is la responsabilidad. After two modules of telling people what to do, the next one is about what we are responsible for.

The grammar is the pair por and para. Notice what that means: no new conjugation, nothing to memorise as a paradigm. You have been using both words since Spanish 1. Módulo 5 sorts out which one goes where, by function — and it does it with conservation writing, which happens to be full of both.

💡 Y LO IMPORTANTE, OTRA VEZ
Módulo 3 told you this and Módulo 4 has now proved it. Every negative tú command you built this week — no hables, no vayas, no seas, no te preocupes, no le escribas — is a subjunctive form. So is every Ud. command from Módulo 3.

Which means that when the present subjunctive arrives in Módulo 7, no new morphology arrives with it. You already produce every form the mood needs, automatically, under pressure, with pronouns attached. What Módulo 7 teaches is when: after quiero que, after es necesario que, after te recomiendo que — the second construction of the three verbs you met on Day 1 of this module.

That is the architecture of this semester, and it is why the commands come first. A student who can say no le escribas without thinking, in October, has already done most of November's work.

🌎 CULTURA · REPÚBLICA DOMINICANA
Lo que te llevas de este módulo

Five things from the Dominican Republic worth keeping. «Dime a ver», a phone greeting that is grammatically a tú command with a pronoun attached. That directness is not discourtesy — plain imperatives between friends read as closeness, and over-softening can create distance. El colmado, the corner shop that also functions as a credit line and an advice bureau. The hermano mayor, whose blunt advice carries real weight, and the remesas that explain why so much of it arrives from abroad. And la bachata, a UNESCO-listed form that was looked down on for decades and now sings in the second person to the whole world.

One last fact, because it reframes the country. Santo Domingo, founded in the 1490s, is the oldest permanent European settlement in the Americas; its Zona Colonial is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and holds the first cathedral built in the hemisphere. And Hispaniola — shared with Haiti — is the only island in the Caribbean divided between two sovereign countries.

So this is not a small island with beaches attached. It is where the European presence in this hemisphere began, and its Spanish is among the oldest continuously spoken in the Americas.

★ LA VIRTUD · EL SERVICIO

Ten days on the grammar of telling people what to do, under the virtue of service — which is less of a contradiction than it looks. Every good adviser in this module did the same three things: they asked before they spoke, they gave a reason, and they stayed available afterwards. The friend awake at midnight, the sibling leaving a note on the fridge, the neighbours leaning on the counter of a colmado, the volunteer explaining which currency to carry. None of them was in charge of anybody. Next module the virtue becomes la responsabilidad, and the question shifts from what you can do for one person to what you are answerable for. Servir es quedarse cuando ya diste el consejo.
