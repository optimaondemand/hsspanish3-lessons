⭐ PREPARACIÓN · eight minutes, now
Reopen 5.01 and 5.02. Play every 🔊 with your eyes closed and say the meaning aloud before you look. Then write these three from memory and check every mark:

la contaminación · el cambio climático · dañar

Three different problems in three words. Contaminación ends in a stressed -ción and the accent is compulsory. Climático is stressed on the third syllable from the end — cli-má-ti-co — and Spanish marks every one of those. And dañar has no accent at all, but it does have ñ, which is a separate letter: «danar» will be marked wrong, and correctly so.

If typing them is awkward, open the Cómo escribir acentos en español page linked from 5.01 and from this item's description in Canvas.

Also worth two minutes: ahorrar and desperdiciar are opposites and appear as each other's distractors, so hearing one and thinking “something to do with using things up” will not be enough.

✍️ PART 4 · LENGUA EN ACCIÓN · 20 pts 11 min

The prompt: Escribe un párrafo sobre una costumbre que has cambiado —o que vas a cambiar— por el medio ambiente, y explica para qué la cambiaste.

Read the prompt's own grammar: it contains a por of cause and a para qué of purpose. The task is built out of the thing it is testing.

RequirementDetail
Length6–8 connected sentences — a paragraph, not a bulleted list
PorAt least three uses doing three different jobs (causa · motivo · duración · recorrido · intercambio · en lugar de alguien)
ParaAt least two uses, one of them para + infinitive of purpose
El par mínimoOne place where the other preposition was also grammatical, and you chose. Be ready to say why.
La razón verdaderaOne sentence that discards a false reason and names the real one: no lo hago por… sino por…
VocabularioFour or more of this module's twenty, accents included
ConectoresFour or more, none repeated
Un mandatoClose with a direct tú command to your reader

One rule about purpose, because it is where this task loses points. Purpose in Spanish 3 is always para + infinitive: para reducir la basura, para proteger el bosque, para no desperdiciar agua. Write it that way every time and this criterion takes care of itself.

✅ PART 5 · ANTES DE ENTREGAR 5 min

One targeted pass, and only one. Go through your paragraph and put a mark over every por and every para. Beside each mark, write the job in one word: recorrido, motivo, duración, intercambio, propósito, plazo, destinatario, en lugar de alguien.

Any preposition you cannot label is the sentence to rewrite. That single check catches nearly everything this task loses points on, and it takes about ninety seconds.

Then submit both items. The Vocabulary Check has a single attempt, so do the eight minutes of listening in Part 3 first, not afterwards.

🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
Doscientos dólares por entrar

In August 2024 Ecuador doubled the Galápagos park entrance fee for adult foreign visitors, from 100 to 200 dollars — the first increase since 1998. Roughly half goes to the municipalities and the province, the rest to the park and conservation work.

That 1998 date matters, because it is the other half of the story. The Ley Especial de Galápagos of that year created the Galápagos Marine Reserve — one of the largest in the world, at around 133 000 km² — reserved the local fishery for island residents, and restricted who may move to the islands at all. Galápagos is a province of Ecuador with about 33 000 inhabitants, and it is one of the few places on earth where you cannot simply decide to live.

Read that as an economics problem and the module's grammar appears on its own. The fee exists para financiar la conservación; it is charged por el daño que trae cada visitante. Forward-looking purpose, backward-looking cause, in the same policy.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA RESPONSABILIDAD

Revising yesterday's paragraph rather than writing a new one is the responsible choice, and not only the easier one: a revised paragraph is one you have actually read, and a fresh draft written at speed is one nobody has. The same logic runs through the entrance fee above — somebody decided that the cost of a place should be paid by the people who impose it. Responder por lo que uno hace empieza por leer lo que uno escribió.
