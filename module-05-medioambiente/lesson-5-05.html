★ Por o para — la pregunta que decide
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Both sentences are correct. That is exactly what makes this hard.
This is the most important lesson in Módulo 5, and the hardest lesson in the semester so far. Not because the forms are difficult — there are no forms. Both words are two syllables and neither one conjugates. It is hard because most of the time both choices produce a grammatical Spanish sentence, and only the meaning changes.

You have spent two days building the two halves of the picture. Today they meet:

Para is an arrow to an endpoint. Por is the ground behind and around the action.

By the end of this lesson you will have one decision question you can run in under a second, seven minimal pairs you know cold, and an honest sense of where the idea stops working.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to apply one decision question to choose between por and para in a new sentence.
2
I will be able to explain what changes in meaning across a minimal pair where both versions are correct.
3
I will be able to write a cause and a purpose in the same sentence about Galápagos without mixing them.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

Two sentences, four words each

Lo hice por ti.
Lo hice para ti.

Both are perfectly good Spanish. A native speaker hears two clearly different situations.

The first says you were the reason — I did it because of you, or in your place, so that you would not have to. The second says you are the destination — I made it and it is now going to you.

Say them aloud, both of them, and notice that nothing about the grammar tells you which to pick. Only the situation does. That is the whole difficulty of this lesson, in eight words.

💡 LA PREGUNTA QUE DECIDE
«¿Estoy señalando un punto final, o estoy describiendo lo de alrededor?»

Point final → para.  Alrededor o detrás → por.

And a faster version for speech, because in conversation you have no time for pictures. Ask which word fits in English:
• because of, in exchange for, through, along, by way of, on behalf of → por
• in order to, headed for, by (this date), destined for, in the view of → para
⚠️ LA HONESTIDAD SOBRE ESTA REGLA
You are owed the truth about this topic, so here it is plainly.

1. No single rule covers por and para perfectly. The arrow-versus-surroundings idea will get you most of the way — considerably further than a memorised list of sixteen uses — but there are sentences where it gives no clear answer, and there are set phrases that simply have to be learned whole: por fin, por supuesto, gracias por, para siempre. Nobody derives those; everybody memorises them.

2. This is the one item in this course that no automatic check can verify. Every other structure you meet this year has a surface pattern a computer can search for — he hablado, había visto, no cruce. Por and para do not. Both are ordinary high-frequency words, both are correct in isolation, and only a human reader can tell whether you chose the right one for what you meant. There is no shortcut and no safety net here: this one is learned by reading a great deal of Spanish and by paying attention when the meaning of a sentence surprises you.

3. That is exactly why it is worth the effort. Choosing correctly between them is one of the clearest signals in the language that a speaker is thinking in Spanish rather than translating into it.

Row 6 is the row to keep. It is the whole conservation register in two sentences, and the Day 8 text is built out of exactly this pair: something exists por a cause and operates para a purpose. If you can produce both halves reliably, you can read almost any Spanish-language conservation document.

📌 DOS QUE NO SE ELIGEN — SE MEMORIZAN
Gracias por venir.  Thanks always name a reason, and a reason is always behind you. Never «gracias para».
Un regalo para mi madre.  A gift has a recipient, and a recipient is where the arrow lands. Never «un regalo por mi madre» — unless you really mean you bought it on her behalf, for someone else.

Learn both whole. They come up constantly, and getting them wrong is immediately audible.

Ahora tú: write three sentences about Galápagos, each containing both words — a cause with por and a purpose with para. Say each one aloud and name the two jobs before you reveal the checklist.

El parque prohíbe la comida en los senderos para no acostumbrar a las aves, porque muchas de ellas anidan en el suelo por la ausencia de depredadores.
Ecuador inspecciona el equipaje en el continente para evitar semillas nuevas, ya que las especies introducidas llegaron por los barcos de carga.
Las islas son únicas por su aislamiento, y hoy ese mismo aislamiento sirve para estudiar la evolución en condiciones casi perfectas.

Comprueba:
• Every para is followed by an infinitive or a noun that the arrow lands on — never a conjugated verb.
• No para que anywhere. If a sentence pushed you toward it, rewrite the sentence.
• Each por can be replaced by a causa de, a lo largo de, a cambio de or en nombre de. If none of them fits, you probably wanted para.
• Read each sentence back and ask whether the meaning you produced is the meaning you intended. That is the only test that exists for this topic.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

Cabras, ratas y una lista incómoda

Las Galápagos entraron en la Lista del Patrimonio Mundial en Peligro en 2007. No fue por un desastre repentino, sino por tres presiones a la vez: el crecimiento del turismo, la inmigración desde el continente y las especies introducidas. Ecuador firmó decretos para limitar la migración y para ordenar el transporte marítimo, y en 2010 la UNESCO sacó al archipiélago de la lista.

El trabajo más difícil fue con los animales introducidos. Los balleneros del siglo XIX dejaron cabras en las islas para tener carne fresca en viajes posteriores. Las cabras se multiplicaron y comieron la vegetación de las zonas secas; las tortugas gigantes se quedaron sin comida. A comienzos de los años dos mil, un proyecto enorme eliminó decenas de miles de cabras de Santiago y del norte de Isabela. La vegetación volvió en pocos años.

Hoy el archipiélago sigue en un equilibrio incómodo. El turismo paga la conservación y a la vez la amenaza. La salida de la lista de 2010 fue una buena noticia, pero varios científicos la consideraron temprana. Para ellos, la presión no había desaparecido: solo se había vuelto más lenta.

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Go through the passage and label every por and every para. There are seven.

Two are worth arguing about, which is the point. «Dejaron cabras para tener carne fresca» — purpose, an arrow into the future: the whalers were planning ahead, and that is what makes the sentence so bleak in hindsight. And «Para ellos, la presión no había desaparecido» — opinion, job 6 from yesterday, aiming the judgement at the people holding it.

Then notice the structure of the second sentence: no fue por un desastre repentino, sino por tres presiones a la vez. «No … sino…» is a construction worth stealing for your Lengua en acción paragraph — it corrects a likely assumption before making its own claim, and it is exactly how a careful writer handles a subject where the easy explanation is wrong.
🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
Cuatro años en la lista roja de la UNESCO

The Galápagos were among the very first sites inscribed on the World Heritage List, in 1978, when the list itself was new. Less famously, they spent 2007 to 2010 on the List of World Heritage in Danger — a designation that carries no legal force but a great deal of embarrassment, and which governments lobby hard to escape.

What is interesting is how it worked. The listing was not a punishment; it was leverage. Ecuador responded with migration controls, tighter shipping rules and a reorganisation of park administration, and UNESCO removed the site in 2010. Several conservation scientists said publicly at the time that the removal was premature and politically driven — and that disagreement is still live.

The goat story is the part that stays with people. Nineteenth-century whalers released goats deliberately, as a living food supply for future voyages. By the 1990s tens of thousands of feral goats had stripped the vegetation the giant tortoises fed on. The eradication campaign of the early 2000s was one of the largest ever attempted on islands of that size, and it worked: the vegetation returned within a few years, and tortoise numbers on several islands have risen since.

The uncomfortable moral is that both the damage and the repair were deliberate human projects, two centuries apart, carried out by people who never met.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA RESPONSABILIDAD

Today's grammar is an unusually exact instrument for thinking about responsibility, because it forces you to separate two things people constantly blur. Por names what has already happened and cannot be undone: the goats were released, the rats came ashore, the seeds arrived in the cargo. Para names what the work is now aimed at. Sitting only with the first produces guilt, which changes nothing; sitting only with the second produces slogans. Stewardship needs both halves in the same sentence — an honest cause and a stated purpose — which is precisely what today's writing task asked you to produce. La causa explica; el propósito obliga.
