★ Por y para en un párrafo — el compromiso, explicado
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Nobody chooses between por and para one blank at a time. You choose while you are making an argument.
Last full practice day. Days 3, 4 and 5 gave you the two prepositions and the minimal pairs; today they stop being an exercise and become prose.

That matters more here than in most modules, because a paragraph about the environment is almost entirely made of causes and purposes. Say what you did, and you need por. Say what it was for, and you need para. A whole paragraph without either is a paragraph that never explains itself.

What you write today is what tomorrow's Lengua en acción (20 pts) asks for. Keep it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to write a 6–8 sentence paragraph in which every por and every para is doing a job I can name.
2
I will be able to spot a preposition that breaks somebody else's argument, inside running prose rather than in a blank.
3
I will be able to explain what Ecuador's 2008 constitution did that no constitution had done before.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

Dos preguntas, y nada de listas

The whole module reduces to two questions, and you ask them of the sentence, never of the word:

PreguntaSi la respuesta es sí…Preposición
¿Apunta hacia adelante — a un destino, un propósito, una fecha o alguien que recibe?Es una flecha hacia un punto finalpara
¿Explica el terreno de atrás — la causa, el motivo, el intercambio, la duración, el recorrido, el medio, en lugar de quién?Cubre el suelo por donde pasó la acciónpor

Notice that neither question mentions English. “For” is not the test and never was — it is the reason this contrast is hard for English speakers in the first place.

🧠 PART 2 · LA ARQUITECTURA DEL PÁRRAFO 10 min

Una forma para un compromiso

Every module gives you a shape rather than a list of sentences to imitate. This one is a compromiso: how you used to be, what changed you, what you do now, and why. Read the third column — the prepositions are not decoration. Each slot in the argument invites one of them.

OraciónSu trabajoQué preposición pide el sentidoCómo empezar

Sentence 6 is the one that marks an Honors writer here. Anybody can list what they do. Saying what you are not doing it for, and then naming the real cause, is an argument — and it is built out of three por phrases in a row. It is also the sentence most students leave out, so write it on purpose.

⭐ LA REGLA DE LOS CONECTORES · still in force
No connector twice. For a compromiso you have: durante años · el año pasado · desde entonces · además · sin embargo · por eso · en cambio · al final · a partir de ahora. Nine, and eight sentences need at most five.

Watch por eso: it is a connector and a por of cause. It counts as both, which makes it the most efficient word in the paragraph.
📚 PART 3 · MODELO ANOTADO 9 min

Mi compromiso, y por qué

1 Durante años pasé por el mismo parque todos los días sin fijarme en la basura de las aceras. 2 El año pasado mi escuela organizó una limpieza del río y me apunté por curiosidad, no por convicción. 3 Trabajamos por tres horas bajo el sol y llenamos veintidós bolsas. 4 Para mí, ese sábado cambió algo: el problema no estaba en las noticias, sino a cien metros de mi casa. 5 Desde entonces he cambiado tres costumbres para reducir mi huella: llevo una botella reutilizable, cambié las bolsas de plástico por una de tela y camino para ir a la escuela. 6 No lo hago por la nota ni por obligación, sino por respeto a un lugar que no es solo mío. 7 Para diciembre quiero convencer a diez compañeros; para lograrlo voy a hablar en la asamblea. 8 Cambia una costumbre esta semana — y hazlo por ti, no por mí.

Fourteen prepositions in eight sentences, and not one of them is there to fill a blank. Here is what each is actually doing.

Two details worth stealing. Sentence 5 puts an intercambio and a propósito side by side in one sentence, which is the clearest way to show a reader you know the difference. And sentence 8 uses hazlo — an affirmative tú command with the pronoun attached, from Módulo 4 — so the paragraph ends by asking something of somebody instead of trailing off.

🧠 PART 4 · PRACTÍCALO 11 min

Three items, none of them a blank in isolation. In each one both prepositions are possible Spanish — what decides is the argument the sentence is inside.

✅ TRY IT · two grammatical options, one meaning
Your paragraph says: «Separo el reciclaje ___ mi hermana pequeña.» You mean that she is the reason — you are thinking about the world she will grow up in. Which one says that?
✅ TRY IT · find the drift
«Camino a la escuela por reducir mi huella.» What has gone wrong?
✅ TRY IT · give each one its own room
A classmate writes «Trabajamos tres horas para la limpieza del parque.» She means the work lasted three hours and that a clean park was the point. Which version says both?

Ahora tú — tomorrow's graded task, rehearsed. Write a 6–8 sentence compromiso following Part 2's shape: a habit you have changed, or are going to change, for the environment. Produce it first, then open the checklist.

Before you call it finished:
• Read every por and para and name its job out loud — recorrido, motivo, duración, intercambio, en lugar de alguien, propósito, plazo, opinión. If you cannot name it, that is the sentence to fix.
• At least three different jobs of por, not the same one three times.
• At least one para + infinitivo of purpose. Purpose in this course is always para + infinitive: para reducir la basura.
• Sentence 6 exists — the real reason, with a discarded one in front of it. Do not skip it.
• One place where you could have written the other preposition, and you chose deliberately.
• Four or more different connectors, none repeated.
• At least four words from this module's twenty, spelled with their accents: la contaminación, el cambio climático, dañar, la huella.
• A direct tú command to your reader in the last sentence.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

El archipiélago y sus visitantes

Las Galápagos están a unos mil kilómetros de la costa de Ecuador. Ese aislamiento explica casi todo: por la distancia, muchas especies llegaron una sola vez y después evolucionaron solas. Hoy alrededor del ochenta por ciento de las aves terrestres del archipiélago no vive en ningún otro lugar del planeta.

El problema moderno no es la distancia, sino el éxito. En 1990 llegaban unos 40 000 visitantes al año; hoy llegan más de 250 000. Cada avión trae dinero para la conservación y también semillas, insectos y basura. Por eso el parque limita el número de barcos, cierra algunos sitios por temporadas y cobra una entrada alta para financiar el control de especies invasoras.

La Estación Científica Charles Darwin, en Puerto Ayora, trabaja en las islas desde 1964. Sus biólogos han criado tortugas en cautiverio para devolverlas después a las islas de donde vienen: un esfuerzo colectivo de sesenta años que ha salvado por lo menos a dos especies del borde de la extinción.

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Nine prepositions, and the paragraph's whole argument sits in them. Every por looks backwards at a cause or a stretch of something — por la distancia (causa), por ciento (proporción, a fixed phrase), por eso (causa), por temporadas (duración), por lo menos (fixed). Every para looks forwards at what the money and the tortoises are for.

Try the deletion test: cross out all nine and read it again. You are left with facts and no argument — which is precisely why this module sits inside the environment theme rather than anywhere else.
🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
Los derechos de la naturaleza — 2008

In September 2008 Ecuador approved a new constitution by referendum, and with it did something no country had done before: it wrote rights of nature into its founding law. Articles 71 to 74 name la naturaleza — also called Pacha Mama, using the Kichwa term — as a holder of rights: to exist, to persist, and to have its cycles restored.

The grammatical consequence is this module's grammar. Because nature cannot go to court itself, the constitution lets any person act por an ecosystem — on its behalf. That is the por of sentence 8 in Part 3, written into law.

Two honest complications, since this module's virtue is responsibility. For over a decade the articles were more quoted abroad than applied at home, and Ecuador remains an oil exporter whose economy pulls against them. Then in December 2021 the Constitutional Court ruled in the Los Cedros cloud-forest case that mining concessions there violated the rights of nature — the first ruling to give the articles real teeth.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA RESPONSABILIDAD

Sentence 6 is a small exercise in responsibility, which is why it is in the shape. Naming what you are not doing something for — not the grade, not the requirement — forces you to find out whether there is a real reason underneath. Sometimes there is not yet, and that is worth knowing too. A habit without a reason is a habit somebody else can talk you out of. La responsabilidad no está en la costumbre, sino en la razón que la sostiene.
