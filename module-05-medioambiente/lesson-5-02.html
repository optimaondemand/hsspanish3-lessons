★ Acciones sostenibles — la forma de un argumento ambiental

Anyone can list problems. An argument has four moves, and most people stop after two.
Yesterday you learned the words for the damage. Today you get the words for doing something about it — ahorrar, reducir, reutilizar — and something more valuable than vocabulary: the four-move shape that an environmental argument takes in Spanish, in English, and in the UNESCO text you will read on Day 8.

Still no new grammar. Everything today runs on the present tense and the perfect tenses you already own. But watch moves 2 and 4 closely: they are exactly where por and para live, and tomorrow the module's grammar begins there.

⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Why most environmental writing is boring

Read enough of it and you notice the same failure again and again: a fact, then an emotion, then nothing. Hay mucho plástico en el mar. Es terrible. Both sentences are true and the paragraph goes nowhere, because it never names a cause and never names a proposal.

Those two moves are what make writing on this subject worth reading, and — usefully for you — they are the two that Spanish marks with a single small word each. Part 3 gives you the shape; Days 3 to 5 give you the words.

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Three Spanish verbs, one English word. English “save” splits three ways and mixing them up is one of the more visible errors an intermediate student makes.
• ahorrar — to use less of a finite thing: ahorrar agua, ahorrar dinero, ahorrar energía.
• guardar — to keep or put away: guardo las botellas para reutilizarlas.
• salvar — to rescue from destruction: salvaron a la tortuga. Strong word; use it for lives, not for water.

Amenazado is a participle used as an adjective, so it agrees — the pattern you learned in Módulo 2: un bosque amenazado, una especie amenazada, dos islas amenazadas. In conservation writing you will also meet en peligro de extinción, which is stronger.

La huella is a footprint or a track in the ground, and by extension la huella ecológica and la huella de carbono. It is also the word in huella digital, a fingerprint. One image, three uses.

El esfuerzo — effort — you already met in Módulo 2, where it belonged to personal achievement. El esfuerzo colectivo is the phrase this module keeps returning to, because that is the honest scale of the problem, and because Galápagos is a real example of it working. Note how little Spanish has to change to move a word from one register to the other: same noun, one adjective, completely different politics.
🧠 PART 3 · LOS CUATRO MOVIMIENTOS 9 min

Four moves, in this order

MoveWhat it doesEjemplo
1. El datoWhat is happening — a fact, not a feelingCada año llegan más de doscientos mil visitantes a las islas.
2. La causaWhy — where por livesEl número ha crecido por la fama del archipiélago.
3. La consecuenciaWhat follows from itPor eso hay más basura, más barcos y más especies introducidas.
4. La propuestaWhat we should do, and what for — where para livesHay que reducir el número de barcos para proteger las costas.

Move 2 is the one that separates an argument from a complaint. A fact plus a proposal with no cause between them is just a preference. And notice that move 4 needs both halves: the action and the purpose. «Hay que reducir el número de barcos» on its own invites the question ¿para qué? — so answer it in the same sentence.

💡 LAS TRES R · Y POR QUÉ ESTÁN EN ESE ORDEN
In Spanish they are reducir, reutilizar, reciclar, and the order is an argument, not a slogan. Reducir comes first because a thing never produced needs no handling at all. Reutilizar comes second because using an object again costs almost nothing. Reciclar comes last because recycling is itself an industrial process: it consumes energy, water and transport.

So when you write move 4, notice which of the three R's you are proposing. A student who proposes recycling is proposing the most expensive of the three — which may still be right, but is worth knowing.
⭐ LA REGLA DE LOS CONECTORES · still in force
No connector twice, the rule since Módulo 0. For an argument you have: en primer lugar · además · por eso · por lo tanto · sin embargo · en cambio · a pesar de esto · al final. Eight, and a six-sentence paragraph needs at most four.
🧠 PART 4 · PRACTÍCALO 8 min
✅ TRY IT · three verbs, one English word
«Si cierras el grifo mientras te lavas los dientes, ___ mucha agua.»
✅ TRY IT · which move is missing?
«Hay plástico en las playas de Galápagos. Llega por las corrientes del Pacífico. Por eso las tortugas lo confunden con comida.» ¿Qué falta?
✅ TRY IT · a participle that agrees
«En el archipiélago hay varias especies ___.» (amenazar)
✅ TRY IT · the order of the three R's
According to the logic in Part 3, which action does the least additional damage?

Ahora tú: write four sentences about a real environmental problem where you live — one for each move, in order. Use at least three words from today's ten. Keep it: you will rebuild this paragraph on Day 6 with the module's grammar.

En mi ciudad se tira mucha comida todos los días. Se desperdicia por costumbre y porque una lechuga cuesta poco. Por eso la basura orgánica llena los camiones y produce gases en el vertedero. En mi casa hemos empezado a comprar menos cantidad cada semana para reducir el desperdicio.

Check yours: four separate moves, in order · the cause is a real cause, not a feeling · the proposal is small enough to be true · and no connector used twice.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

Treinta mil personas y doscientos setenta mil visitantes

En los años cincuenta casi nadie vivía en las Galápagos. Hoy viven allí unas treinta mil personas y llegan cada año muchos más visitantes que residentes: alrededor de doscientos setenta mil antes de la pandemia.

El turismo no es un enemigo simple. Paga los sueldos de los guardaparques, financia parte de la investigación científica y da trabajo a familias enteras. Pero cada avión, cada barco y cada caja de fruta traen algo más: semillas, insectos, ratas. Las especies introducidas han hecho más daño al archipiélago que la contaminación.

Por eso Ecuador controla las islas como pocos lugares del mundo. Hay inspección de equipaje al salir del continente, no solo al llegar. Los visitantes recorren los sitios con un guía autorizado y por rutas marcadas. La residencia está limitada por ley. Y desde 1998 existe una reserva marina enorme alrededor de las islas, ampliada de nuevo en 2022.

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Find the four moves. Paragraph 1 is el dato. Paragraph 2 gives la causa and refuses the easy version of it — notice the sentence «el turismo no es un enemigo simple», which concedes something before it argues. Paragraph 3 is la consecuencia and la propuesta at once, because in this case the proposals already exist and are being carried out.

Then count the por: por rutas marcadas, por ley, por eso. Three different jobs again, and tomorrow you will be able to name all three.
🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
El parque de 1959 y el 97 %

Ecuador created the Parque Nacional Galápagos in 1959 — the country's first national park, declared in the centenary year of Origin of Species. The park covers roughly 97 % of the archipelago's land area. The remaining 3 % is where all thirty thousand residents live, on four inhabited islands.

That ratio is the whole management problem in one number. It is not a wilderness with nobody in it; it is a wilderness with a town inside, and the town needs schools, fuel, cargo ships and jobs. In 1998 Ecuador added the Reserva Marina de Galápagos, about 133,000 km² of ocean, and in 2022 declared a further marine corridor northwards toward Costa Rica's Isla del Coco, along the route migrating sharks and turtles actually swim.

Worth noticing: the biosecurity inspection happens at Quito and Guayaquil airports, before you fly, because the point is to stop a seed arriving rather than to catch it afterwards. That is a genuinely unusual piece of policy design, and it is what stewardship looks like when it is taken seriously rather than announced.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA RESPONSABILIDAD

Move 4 is where responsibility enters the paragraph, and it is why the assignment asks for a proposal small enough to be true. A grand proposal costs the writer nothing; a small one commits them. The Galápagos luggage inspection is not a heroic act, it is a queue at an airport — and it is one of the most effective conservation measures in the Pacific. Stewardship is usually like that: unglamorous, repeated, and carried out by people whose names nobody knows. La responsabilidad empieza donde termina la queja.
