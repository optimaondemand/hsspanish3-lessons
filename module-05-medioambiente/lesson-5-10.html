Two things about its design, so nothing is a surprise. Every item is a sentence, not a list — there is no question anywhere that asks you to recite what por means. And the listening item is a pair of short sentences that differ in exactly one word, the preposition; you choose which meaning you heard. That is why Part 4 of yesterday's lesson spent eleven minutes on two syllables.

⭐ CÓMO USAR DOS INTENTOS
Take attempt 1 seriously — its value is telling you which of the eleven rows above is weak — then reopen that lesson before attempt 2. Students who take both attempts back to back usually score about the same twice, having learned nothing in between.

For this module specifically: if you lost points on the signal-free items, do not reread the lists. Reread 5.05's minimal pairs and Part 3 below, because the problem is not what the words mean, it is deciding what you mean before you choose.

➡️ PART 5 · LO QUE VIENE 8 min

Módulo 6 — del medio ambiente a las artes

Módulo 6 changes almost everything on the surface. The theme becomes las artes y la cultura, the country becomes España, the painter is Diego Velázquez, and the virtue is la honestidad — honesty, which is exactly the right virtue for a module about looking at a painting properly, since looking properly means saying what is really there and admitting what you cannot tell.

The grammar is the family of small words that join one clause to another: que, quien, lo que, cuyo. You have been using plain que since Spanish 1 without thinking about it — «el parque que abarca casi ocho mil kilómetros cuadrados» — and Módulo 6 teaches the rest of the family, which is what lets you describe something complicated in one sentence instead of four short ones. Describing art is where that pays off immediately, because a painting cannot be described without constantly saying which figure, whose hand, what the light is doing.

💡 Y LO QUE NO CAMBIA
This module does not close. Por and para now belong to you and Módulo 6 will use them constantly, because writing about art runs on purpose and cause just as much as writing about conservation does.

And you have already met the single most common por in the whole of art writing. In 5.08 you worked out why the encyclopedia said consideradas por expertos y científicos — the agent, whoever performs the action. That is the same por as «Las meninas, pintado por Velázquez en 1656». Every museum label in Spanish is built on it, and you did not learn it from a list. You worked it out from the idea that por covers the ground behind the action.

Módulo 6's reading is the Spanish encyclopedia entry on Las meninas. Velázquez died in 1660, so the painting is fully in the public domain — which means that for the first time this semester you will be looking at the artwork itself, not a description of it.

🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
Lo que te llevas de este módulo

Four things from Ecuador worth keeping. 1959: the Parque Nacional Galápagos, the country's first, created in the centenary year of El origen de las especies, and today covering 97 % of the archipelago's land area. 2008: the first constitution in the world to grant rights to nature, which is why any citizen may go to court por an ecosystem — and the Los Cedros ruling of 2021, which finally gave those articles force.

The population figure that explains the policy: Galápagos is a province of about 33 000 people, in a country of some 18 million, receiving over 250 000 visitors a year. Residency is restricted, the fishery is reserved for locals, and the park entrance fee doubled in 2024. None of that is ordinary tourism management; it is a place run on the assumption that it can be used up.

And the sixty-year programme at the Charles Darwin Station: Lonesome George, who ran out of time, and Diego, who did not. Ecuador is also mainland — Quito's colonial centre, the Amazon, the Andes — and Galápagos is one of its four regions, not the whole country. But it is the one where the argument between using a place and keeping it is most visible, which is why this module lives there.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA RESPONSABILIDAD

Ten days on two prepositions, in a module whose virtue is stewardship — which is less of a coincidence than it looks. Por and para are the words a language uses to ask why and what for, and responsibility is almost entirely a matter of being able to answer those two questions about your own behaviour. A habit you cannot explain is not yet a commitment; a rule imposed without a purpose is not yet stewardship. Everything this module asked you to write — the real-reason sentence, the closing command spoken on somebody else's behalf — was practice at answering. Next module the virtue becomes la honestidad, and the first thing it asks of you is to describe what is actually in a painting rather than what you expected to find there. Responder por algo empieza por saber para qué lo haces.
