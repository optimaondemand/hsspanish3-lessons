🌎 CULTURA · MÉXICO
¿Por qué el acento importa aquí?

A practical reason the written accent is graded rather than excused: Spanish is the official language of twenty-one countries, and its spelling is agreed collectively by the Real Academia Española together with twenty-two sister academies across the Americas — including the Academia Mexicana de la Lengua, founded in 1875.

So when you write el estómago with its tilde, you are not satisfying a teacher's preference; you are following a rule that Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Spain all agreed on. There is no regional variation to hide behind. It is one of the few areas of the language where the standard really is the standard everywhere.

★ LA VIRTUD · EL AUTOGOBIERNO

Graded work opens today, and from here the schedule, the drafts and the deadline are yours to run — which is what self-government costs in practice. Revising rather than rewriting is the self-governed choice, and the harder one: it means facing what you actually produced instead of starting fresh where everything still feels possible. Opening yesterday's paragraph and fixing three accents is less satisfying than a new draft and worth considerably more. Corregir cuesta más que empezar, y enseña más.
