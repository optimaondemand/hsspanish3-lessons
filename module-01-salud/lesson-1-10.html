⭐ CÓMO USAR DOS INTENTOS · the part students get wrong
Two attempts is not permission to guess through the first. Take attempt 1 seriously — its value is telling you which of the eleven areas above is weak — then reopen that lesson before attempt 2. ➡️ PART 5 · LO QUE VIENE 8 min

Why Module 1 had to come first

Module 2 introduces the past perfect: había hablado — “I had spoken.” It is the same two-piece machine you built this week, with haber in the imperfect instead of the present. Everything you learned about the participle, the pronoun order and the unsplittable pair transfers unchanged.

Module 2 also finally gives you the irregular participles you have been carefully avoiding: hecho, dicho, visto, escrito, puesto, abierto, vuelto, muerto. They were held back on purpose — a rule is easier to own when it is not competing with a list of exceptions in the same week.

And the theme changes to experiencias y logros — experiences and achievements — with Costa Rica as the featured culture and la perseverancia as the virtue. The past perfect turns out to be the tense achievement is described in: nunca antes había logrado…

🌎 CULTURA · MÉXICO
Lo que te llevas de este módulo

Two things from México worth keeping. First, the practical one: the pharmacy with a consultorio attached, and the fact that «voy a la farmacia» can mean I'm going to see a doctor. That is genuinely useful if you are ever unwell in Mexico.

Second, the deeper one: la medicina tradicional is not folklore that survives despite modern medicine — it runs alongside it, in most families, without contradiction, and the knowledge is held in sixty-eight languages besides Spanish. The grandmother's line from Day 6 is the best summary anyone has given you: «no curan todo, pero acompañan».

★ LA VIRTUD · LA TEMPLANZA

Ten days on a topic where the temperate answer is almost always the right one: rest before panic, ask before enduring, report accurately rather than dramatically. La templanza is unglamorous and it is what keeps a body and a semester running. Next module the virtue becomes la perseverancia — which is temperance with a longer horizon. Primero la medida, después la constancia.
