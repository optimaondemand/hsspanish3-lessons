Lesson 7.05
★ El marco impersonal — es necesario que, es importante que, es mejor que
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Advice with nobody's name on it. The clause stays; the wanter disappears.
Every trigger you have used so far has an owner. Quiero — I want. Te recomiendo — I advise. Somebody is pushing, and that somebody is you.

Today the first half of the sentence stops naming anyone: es necesario que, es importante que, es mejor que. The push is still there, but it comes from the situation rather than from a person — and that turns out to be the most useful register in the module, because it lets you advise a stranger, a teacher or an employer without putting your own will on the table.

The rule from Día 3 survives intact. What needs a second version is the question, and the second version is just as quick.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use es necesario / es importante / es mejor / es imprescindible with a que clause in the subjunctive.
2
I will be able to choose between the frame + infinitive (true for everybody) and the frame + que + subjunctive (aimed at one person).
3
I will be able to explain what changes socially when the advice stops belonging to me.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Un consejo sin dueño

Four ways to tell somebody to rest a day. Same advice, four different amounts of you in it:

«Descansa un día.»  →  a command. The push is mine and it lands on you. (Módulo 4)
«Quiero que descanses un día.»  →  my wish, with my name on it.
«Te recomiendo que descanses un día.»  →  my judgement, offered rather than imposed.
«Es mejor que descanses un día.»  →  nobody's wish. The altitude's verdict.

Notice what vanished on the last rung: me. The verb form after que never changed — descanses throughout — but the sentence stopped being about what I want. That is the whole of today, and it is more social than grammatical.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which of those four would you rather be given by somebody you had just met?
🧠 PART 2 · EL MARCO IMPERSONAL 11 min

A frame with a slot in it

Es + adjective + que. The adjective is the slot, and this fortnight you fill it with four words — three from today plus imprescindible, which you already learned on Día 1.

El marcoPara todo el mundo · infinitivoPara una persona · que + subjuntivo
es necesarioEs necesario reservar con antelación.Es necesario que reserves con antelación.
es importanteEs importante beber mucha agua.Es importante que bebas mucha agua.
es mejorEs mejor no subir el mismo día.Es mejor que no subas el mismo día.
es imprescindibleEs imprescindible llevar el pasaporte.Es imprescindible que lleves el pasaporte.
  • Es necesario que llegues temprano.the frame, aimed at one person
  • Quiero que estudies.compare: here the wanter is named
  • Recomiendo que lo intentes.the middle rung of Part 1's ladder
💡 LA PREGUNTA, SEGUNDA VERSIÓN
Día 3 gave you the test: is the person who wants also the person who acts? In this frame there is no person who wants, so that question has nothing to bite on. Ask this instead:

¿Va dirigido a alguien en concreto? — Is this aimed at somebody in particular?
• No, it is true for anybody → Es necesario reservar con antelación.
• Sí, there is a named or implied person → Es necesario que reserves con antelación.

And the original rule still holds if you count honestly: the impersonal frame has zero subjects in the first half, so any person named in the second half is automatically a different subject from nobody. Two subjects, one of them absent. Same rule, same clause.

Both columns are correct Spanish. The difference is not accuracy, it is scope — a rule for everyone versus an instruction for you. Signs, laws and recipes live in the left column. Conversations live in the right.

🧠 PART 3 · DOS FRONTERAS 9 min

Where the frame stops, and what it buys you

⚠️ FRONTERA 1 · NO TODO «ES… QUE» EMPUJA
Put a different kind of adjective in the slot and the sentence stops pushing and starts reporting — so it takes the indicative, exactly like Día 1's «la ventaja es que…»:

«Es verdad que Puno está a más de tres mil ochocientos metros.»
«Está claro que el sol de la sierra quema más de lo que parece.»

Neither of those is trying to move anybody; both are claims about the world. The test is the one from Día 1 and it has not changed: ¿informo o empujo? Necesario, importante, mejor, imprescindible push — they say something ought to happen. Verdad and claro report.

This is the same lesson in a new costume: que triggers nothing. What is in front of it decides.
👉 FRONTERA 2 · LA ESCALERA COMPLETA
You now hold four instruments for the same act, and they differ in one dimension: how visible the speaker is.
Instrumento¿Quién empuja?Cuándo conviene
Descansa hoy.yo, directamenteUrgency, or people who know each other well
Quiero que descanses hoy.yo, con mi nombre puestoWhen the relationship can carry your will
Te recomiendo que descanses hoy.yo, ofreciendoAdvice with a reason behind it
Es mejor que descanses hoy.nadie; la situaciónDistance, courtesy, rules, advice upwards
And note that es mejor que plus no is the gentlest way in the language to tell somebody not to do something: «Es mejor que no subas hoy» against «no subas hoy». Identical verb form — subas, which you first wrote as a negative tú command in Módulo 4 — and a completely different social act. The forms have not moved all fortnight. Only their address keeps changing.
🧠 PART 4 · PRACTÍCALO 9 min
✅ TRY IT · ¿a quién va dirigido?
A printed notice at a boat dock, addressed to every passenger. How is it written?
✅ TRY IT · la forma
Complete it: «Es mejor que ustedes ___ mañana temprano.» (ir)
✅ TRY IT · informar o empujar
Which of these is reporting, and therefore takes the indicative?
✅ TRY IT · el instrumento correcto
You barely know a classmate and you want to suggest that the group meet earlier. Which lands best?

Ahora tú: here are three instructions from a sign, written for everybody. Rewrite each one aimed at one person, using a different frame each time — and then take one wish of your own, «quiero que…», and soften it into the impersonal frame.

No dejar basura. ··· Prohibido nadar aquí. ··· Llevar chaleco salvavidas.

—Es importante que no dejes basura; aquí no pasa ningún camión. Es mejor que no nades en esta parte, porque el agua está a diez grados. Y es imprescindible que lleves chaleco durante toda la travesía.

—Quiero que descanses hoy. → Es mejor que descanses hoy.

Check yours: three different adjectives in the slot, three subjunctive clauses (dejes, nades, lleves), and each one carries a reason. The frame is impersonal, not empty — «es mejor que no nades» without «porque el agua está a diez grados» sounds like an arbitrary rule.

Then read the last pair aloud, one after the other, and listen to what changed. The verb did not. Descanses in both. What moved was who is responsible for the advice.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

En el muelle de Puno

Aviso en la caseta de venta:
Es necesario presentar el boleto antes de subir a la lancha. Es obligatorio usar chaleco salvavidas durante toda la travesía. Es importante no ponerse de pie cuando la lancha está en movimiento.

Y el lanchero, ya en el muelle:
—Ustedes dos, atrás. Es mejor que se sienten en la parte cubierta; a esta hora el sol quema y el aire engaña.
—¿Cuánto dura?
—Media hora hasta las islas. Es importante que lleven algo en la cabeza; a tres mil ochocientos metros el sol no perdona.
—¿Y podemos caminar por la isla?
—Sí, pero es necesario que pisen donde pisa la familia que los recibe. El suelo es totora viva; en algunas partes cede. Yo prefiero avisar primero, porque arreglar después sale más caro.
—¿Se puede comprar algo allá?
—Claro. Y les pido una cosa: si compran, compren a la familia que los invitó a su isla, no en la tienda del muelle. Es mejor que el dinero llegue donde está el trabajo.

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Read the notice and the boatman one after the other and the lesson is complete without further explanation.

The notice uses the same four frames you learned today, and every single one is followed by an infinitive — presentar, usar, ponerse. It is printed for whoever turns up; it cannot name anybody.

The boatman uses the same frames and every one opens a clause — que se sienten, que lleven, que pisen, que el dinero llegue. He is looking at four specific people. Same four words, two audiences, two constructions.

Three details worth keeping. «Que se sienten» puts the reflexive pronoun before the verb, in the clause slot from Día 4. «Yo prefiero avisar primero» drops back to an infinitive because the subject stops changing — he warns, he warns. And the last line names a subject that is not a person at all: «es mejor que el dinero llegue…». The clause needs a different subject from the frame, not necessarily a human one.

One spiral: «la familia que los recibe» and «la familia que los invitó» are Módulo 6's relative que — and neither takes the subjunctive, because neither is pushing anything. Módulo 6's que and Módulo 7's que are different words doing different jobs, and this passage has both in one sentence.
🌎 CULTURA · PERÚ
Las islas que hay que volver a construir

El lago Titicaca sits at about 3,810 metres on the border between Peru and Bolivia — the largest lake in South America by volume, and high enough that visitors arriving from the coast feel it within the hour. In the bay near Puno live the Uros, on islands they build themselves out of totora, the tall reed that grows in the shallows.

The engineering is the interesting part. A floating island is a raft of cut reed laid over blocks of the root mass, anchored to the lake bed with stakes — and the bottom layer is permanently rotting. So new reed has to be laid on top, over and over, for as long as anyone wants to keep living there. Nobody repairs an Uros island once it fails; the work is continuous, and a floor that is allowed to thin will simply let a foot through. The same reed is used for the boats, for the houses, and, peeled, is eaten.

Two honest notes, because this course does not sell you a postcard. The Uros' island-building almost certainly began as a way of staying out of reach of stronger neighbours, including the Inca — mobility as defence. And today most families in the bay live substantially on tourism, receiving visitors in rotation on islands maintained partly for that purpose. That is not a fake; it is a real place with a real economy, and it is worth knowing which is which before you photograph it.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA PRUDENCIA

The boatman never once tells anybody what he wants. He says es mejor, es importante, es necesario — and every instruction has a reason attached to it. Four strangers get told where to sit, what to wear and where to walk, and nobody has been ordered about.

That is what the impersonal frame is for, and it is la prudencia in a grammatical structure: it leaves the other person room to weigh the advice instead of having to weigh you. Prudence here is not softness. The advice is firm — the floor really does give way — and it is aimed at the situation rather than at the person's judgement. Like the reed, it is laid down before the failure, not after. Es mejor avisar a tiempo que tener razón después.
