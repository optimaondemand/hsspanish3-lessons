⭐ PREPARACIÓN · eight minutes, now
Reopen 7.01 Part 2 and 7.02 Part 2. Play every 🔊 with your eyes closed and say the meaning aloud before you look at the English. Then write these three from memory and check them letter by letter: la decisión · la precaución · exigir.

Two of those are accent problems and the third is not. Exigir is spelled with a g, not a j — the sound is identical, so only knowing the word helps you. And watch la desventaja in the listening items: Spanish b and v sound the same, so «desbentaja» is exactly what your ear will let you write.

⚠️ EL ERROR QUE ESTA TAREA BUSCA
There are two ways to lose points here and they are opposites.

One: carrying the English structure across — «quiero Mateo llevar el pasaporte», «te recomiendo tú descansar». Spanish has no such pattern. Two people means que and a conjugated verb, every time.

Two, and this is the one nobody expects: putting a subjunctive everywhere because the module is about the subjunctive. «Quiero que yo llegue temprano» is not a sentence — you want, you arrive, one subject, so it is quiero llegar temprano. Reread your draft once looking only at the subjects of each pair of verbs. Where they match, the infinitive is not optional; it is the only correct choice.

🌎 CULTURA · PERÚ
Arequipa, la ciudad construida con un volcán

Mateo's scholarship takes him to Arequipa, Peru's second city, in the south at about 2,300 metres, with the volcano El Misti standing over it. Its colonial centre is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and it is white — hence la Ciudad Blanca.

The whiteness is not paint. Arequipa is built out of el sillar, a pale volcanic rock left by ancient eruptions, quarried on the edge of the city and cut into blocks. It is light, soft enough to carve with hand tools, and it turns a warm cream in low sun. That single material shaped how the city looks: because the ground shakes often, arequipeño builders used thick sillar walls and low vaulted ceilings rather than height, and the buildings that came through the big earthquakes are the ones built that way. Prudence, in the form of architecture.

The strangest thing to visit there is the Monasterio de Santa Catalina, founded in 1579 — not a building but a walled quarter of the city, twenty thousand square metres of streets, plazas and cells, painted in deep red and blue, closed to outsiders for almost four hundred years and opened only in 1970. A city inside a city, with its own street names.

Arequipeño regional pride is proverbial in Peru and locals joke about needing a passport to enter. Ask about el rocoto relleno — a stuffed pepper that looks harmless and is not.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA PRUDENCIA

Today asks you to advise a friend who is about to do something difficult, and the temptation is to say everything you know. Prudence is the editing. Mateo cannot carry twelve warnings; he can carry two, and the two you choose say more about your judgement than the ten you leave out.

The grammar is doing the same work. Quiero que lleves una copia del pasaporte leaves him room to answer; lleva una copia does not. Neither is wrong — Módulo 3 spent ten days on the second one — and choosing between them is not a grammatical decision at all. It is a judgement about the person in front of you. Aconsejar bien es escoger qué callar.
