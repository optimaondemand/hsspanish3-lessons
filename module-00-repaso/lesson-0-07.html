✅ PART 5 · ANTES DE ENTREGAR 5 min

Read your paragraph out loud before submitting. It is the fastest proofreading tool you have: a missing verb, a tense that fights its sentence, and a repeated connector are all audible even when they are invisible on the page.

Then submit both items. If you finish early, do not go looking for extra work — go back and play the audio from 0.01 once more. The Vocabulary Check is the only item this module with a single attempt.

🌎 CULTURA · EL MUNDO HISPANO
Por qué el oído va primero

The Vocabulary Check is ear-first for a reason that is cultural, not merely pedagogical. Spanish is spoken across twenty-one countries with genuinely different rhythms: Caribbean Spanish often drops final s, so los dos can arrive as lo' do'; Argentine Spanish pronounces ll and y as a sh sound; central Mexican Spanish is comparatively even and clear, which is why it is used for the audio in this course.

If you only ever meet a word in writing, you have learned one version of it — the version nobody says. Training your ear on clear es-MX first gives you a stable centre from which the other accents become variations rather than obstacles.

★ LA VIRTUD · LAS SIETE VIRTUDES

Graded work opens today, and with it the fifth of the seven: responsibility (la responsabilidad) — the plain fact that this paragraph is yours and nobody else is going to find the missing accents in it. There is a particular kind of responsibility in revising rather than rewriting: it means facing what you actually produced instead of starting fresh where everything still feels possible. Opening yesterday's paragraph and fixing three accents is less satisfying than writing a new one, and it is worth far more. Corregir es más difícil que empezar, y enseña más.
