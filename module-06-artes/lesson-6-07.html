🌎 CULTURA · ESPAÑA
El cuadro que cambió de nombre

The most famous painting in Spain was not called Las meninas for the first two centuries of its life. In the royal inventories it was La familia del señor rey Felipe IV — a description, not a title, because paintings in a private collection did not need marketable names.

“Las meninas” came from a museum catalogue. The Prado's nineteenth-century cataloguers used it, it stuck, and by the time the painting was world-famous the nickname had replaced the description entirely. Tomorrow's reading records both names and dates each one, which is why it is worth knowing now.

And menina is not originally a Spanish word. It is Portuguese for a girl, taken into the Spanish court's vocabulary for the young noblewomen who attended the royal children — the two girls in the painting are Isabel de Velasco and María Agustina Sarmiento. So Spain's national painting is known by a Portuguese loanword applied by a nineteenth-century cataloguer to a seventeenth-century picture. Titles are historical objects too.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA ATENCIÓN

Revising yesterday's paragraph rather than writing a new one is the harder discipline, and it is the one this module's virtue actually names. To revise you have to look at your own sentences as they are, not as you meant them — and that is uncomfortable in a way that starting fresh is not. Every writer knows the temptation to rewrite instead of read. La atención is the refusal of it. Corregir es volver a mirar lo que ya creías terminado.
