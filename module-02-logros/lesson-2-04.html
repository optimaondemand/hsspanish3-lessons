Lesson 2.04
★ Los participios en acción — el segundo trabajo
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One word, two jobs — and the test for which is simply: is haber there?
You met this in Module 1 through a real text: «territorio entendido…», where the participle agreed because it was working as an adjective. Today you own it properly, with the eleven irregular participles you learned yesterday.

It matters more than it sounds. Get this wrong and you write «la puerta ha abierta» or «las inscripciones están abierto» — two errors that mark a writer as uncertain about something basic.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to apply the haber test to decide whether a participle agrees.
2
I will be able to use estar + participle to describe a resulting state.
3
I will be able to use all eleven irregular participles in both jobs.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The action and the result

«Alguien ha abierto la ventana.» — the action
«La ventana está abierta.» — the result

Same participle, two completely different jobs. The first reports something that happened; the second describes how things are now. And notice the agreement: abierto in the first (fixed), abierta in the second (agreeing with ventana).

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which pair of sentences would you be more likely to need in a real conversation?
🧠 PART 2 · LA PRUEBA DE HABER 10 min

One question decides it every time

¿Hay haber? If yes, the participle is frozen in -o. If no, it is an adjective and must agree.

With haber — the actionWithout haber — the state
AgreementNever — always -oAlways — matches the noun
abrirHan abierto las inscripciones.Las inscripciones están abiertas.
escribirHa escrito las cartas.Las cartas ya están escritas.
resolverHemos resuelto el problema.El problema está resuelto.
romperAlguien ha roto las sillas.Las sillas están rotas.
hacerHe hecho la tarea.La tarea está hecha.

Read down the right-hand column and hear how ordinary those sentences are — está cerrado, está hecha, está resuelto. This is not an advanced flourish; it is everyday Spanish, and it is the half of the participle that English handles with the same word (“the door is closed”) so it rarely gets taught explicitly.

⭐ UNA TERCERA POSICIÓN · the bare adjective
Participles also sit directly on a noun with no verb at all — exactly what the Module 1 text did with «territorio entendido»:

una oportunidad perdida · un proyecto propuesto · las metas alcanzadas · un premio merecido

No haber, so they agree. Same rule, no new test needed.
🧠 PART 3 · ESTAR + PARTICIPIO 10 min

Describing where things stand

This construction is how Spanish reports the current state of a process — enormously useful when you are describing progress, which is this module's whole theme.

What you want to saySpanish
The application is finishedLa solicitud está terminada.
Registration is open / closedLa inscripción está abierta / cerrada.
The essays are writtenLos ensayos están escritos.
The problem is solvedEl problema está resuelto.
I am tired / I am sittingEstoy cansado/a / sentado/a.

Note the last row: cansado and sentado are participles too, and you have been agreeing them correctly since Spanish 1 without knowing what they were. You already do this — today you find out why.

⚠️ NO CONFUNDIR
«La tienda ha cerrado» = the shop has closed (an action, maybe permanently — it went out of business).
«La tienda está cerrada» = the shop is closed (a state — come back tomorrow).

Getting these two mixed up genuinely changes the message. This is one of the few places in the module where the wrong choice is not merely untidy but misleading.
🧠 PART 4 · PRACTÍCALO 10 min
✅ TRY IT · apply the haber test
«Las inscripciones ya están ___ .» (abrir)
✅ TRY IT · haber is there
«Mis profesoras han ___ las cartas.» (escribir)
✅ TRY IT · action or state?
You arrive at the office at 8 p.m. and the door will not open. What do you tell your friend?
✅ TRY IT · bare adjective on a noun
«Fue una oportunidad ___ .» (perder — a lost opportunity)

Ahora tú: describe the state of four things around you using estar + participle, and make at least two of them use an irregular participle from 2.03.

La puerta está abierta. Mi tarea de matemáticas ya está hecha, pero el ensayo de historia no está terminado. Dos ventanas están rotas en el gimnasio. Y estoy sentado en la biblioteca porque estoy muy cansado.

Check every agreement against its noun: puerta abierta (f.s.) · tarea hecha (f.s.) · ensayo terminado (m.s.) · ventanas rotas (f.pl.) · sentado, cansado (matching the speaker).
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

Estado de la solicitud

Portal de becas — resumen de tu expediente

Formulario principal: completado.
Carta de motivación: recibida el 14 de marzo.
Cartas de recomendación: dos recibidas, una pendiente.
Certificado de notas: no entregado.
Inscripción: abierta hasta el 30 de marzo.

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Sort every bolded word into the two jobs. The list items are all states and every one agrees with its noun — carta recibida, cartas recibidas, certificado entregado, inscripción abierta. The committee's note switches to actions with haber, and there every participle is frozen: revisado, visto, propuesto. Then find the one that goes back to a state: tu carta está muy bien escrita.

A real portal page, doing both jobs in eight lines. This is why the distinction is worth ten minutes.
🌎 CULTURA · COSTA RICA
El lenguaje de los trámites

The word for bureaucratic paperwork across the Spanish-speaking world is los trámites, and it is a word with feeling attached — «estoy haciendo trámites» carries a sigh with it. Costa Rica has invested heavily in moving trámites online, and a scholarship portal like the one above is a genuine document type you would meet.

The reason it is worth reading in this module is grammatical as much as cultural: trámites language runs almost entirely on participles, because its whole job is reporting the state of things — recibido, entregado, pendiente, aprobado, rechazado. If you can read a status page in Spanish, you can handle a large share of institutional life.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA PERSEVERANCIA

The status page says one document is missing and the deadline is not closed yet. That is the ordinary shape of perseverance: not a dramatic setback, just one more thing to upload before the thirtieth. Most persistence is administrative. Lo que falta todavía se puede entregar; eso es perseverar.
