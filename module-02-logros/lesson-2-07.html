🌎 CULTURA · COSTA RICA
Contar tu trayectoria en voz alta

One cultural note about the task you are submitting. In much of Latin America, describing your own achievements is expected to include the people who helped — mi profesora me ayudó, mi familia me apoyó — rather than presenting the path as purely individual. A trayectoria told with nobody else in it can read as slightly cold.

US students often write these paragraphs as solo achievement narratives because that is the register of an American college essay. Neither is wrong, but knowing the difference is part of writing for a Spanish-speaking reader. If someone helped you, say so — it will make the paragraph land better, not weaker.

★ LA VIRTUD · LA PERSEVERANCIA

Revising yesterday's paragraph instead of writing a fresh one is itself the module's virtue in miniature: returning to something already begun, when starting over would feel better. La perseverancia is unglamorous by definition — if it felt impressive while you were doing it, it would probably be something else. Volver al mismo párrafo es más difícil que escribir otro.
